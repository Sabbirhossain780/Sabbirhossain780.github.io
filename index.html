Research & Work Experience

Graduate Researcher (MS Thesis)
Sept. 2024 – Present
Department of CSE, BUET, Dhaka, Bangladesh
  • Developing a transformer-based multivariate time series framework for sparse multi-type event streams, factorizing prediction into total volume and event composition, with cross-category and multi-scale seasonal attention.
  • Designed a hierarchical signal structure for rare event types, motivated by an identifiability analysis of per-cell data support. It combines semantic type grouping in cross-category attention with a support-adaptive prior that weights local evidence against pooled signals.
